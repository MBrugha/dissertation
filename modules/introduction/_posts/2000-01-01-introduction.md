## About my research:
My PhD explores the intersection of online learning and dialogic pedagogy to enhance teacher professional development (TPD). We know that dialogic pedagogy and methods lead to improved learner performance. We also know that TPD is crucial for strengthening the quality of teaching and through this, the educational outcomes of learners. This all signifies that effective TPD programmes regarding educational dialogue are necessary to develop and trial to promote this pedagogy. Enter technology and the MOOC platform. Can we hack the MOOC model to offer new forms of dialogue and interaction between educators pursuing TPD? Can MOOCs broaden access to learning? And is this model sustainable and scalable?

To explore these questions, I designed and trialled three MOOCs that drew on the conceptual model of mechanical MOOCs (more on this in the next module!). This intervention and the analysis of the data from it allowed me to address gaps in research regarding teacher learning in online TPD, design considerations and principles in online TPD, and the impact of online TPD on teacher practice.

The output of my research is an iteratively produced and pedagogically effective TPD MOOC series prototype on educational dialogue that is relevant for a wide range of in-service school-level and higher education educators across varied geographies, along with the lessons for designers and educators learned from trialling it.

## About this course:
This online course has been developed to house the findings of this study in an effort to produce and disseminate open, accessible, and easily navigable and usable results. Since this research explored how to design an online course, it felt fitting that the findings be presented in a course format - i.e. a course on how to design, facilitate and evaluate a course. This online course offers the traditional sections of my dissertation but in a format more appropriate to an online platform and course structure. There are modules that aim to provide a comprehensive overview of my lessons learned in order for you to build on these in your own research and course design. This course also includes reflective prompts throughout the pages of content. These were used for my own reflective logs throughout the entirety of my PhD journey, and are offered here as a resource while you engage with the research findings.

The [Theory and Evidence module](https://mbrugha.github.io/dissertation/modules/theory%20&%20evidence/theory-&-evidence/) presents you with the literature and evidence in the sector. This includes information regarding the process for you to consider how to conduct a similar review or build on the current one.

The [Research Methods module](https://mbrugha.github.io/dissertation/modules/research%20methods/research-methods/) provides an overview of the methodology utilised for the study, including the stages of data collection and analysis, ethical considerations, and limitations of the research. The objective of this module is to critically engage with strengths and limitations of different methods in order to select appropriate ones for your research.

The [Intervention module](https://mbrugha.github.io/dissertation/modules/intervention/trial1/) provides information regarding how to design and trial an intervention of this nature, including important considerations regarding the conceptual framework, structure and content.

The [Findings module](https://mbrugha.github.io/dissertation/modules/findings/findings/) presents the formation, trial and refinement of the design principles, followed by three thematic inquiries. The objective of this module is to critique the findings and plan ways in which they can be leveraged or built on.

The final module offers [conclusions and next steps](https://mbrugha.github.io/dissertation/modules/conclusions/conclusions/), which include a summary of key findings, implications of the research findings for the sector, and recommendations regarding future research directions. The objective is to wrap up your learning from the course and make a plan for next steps.
