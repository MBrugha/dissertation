# Findings

Findings are presented in this module for you to review, critique, and consider ways to build on them. There are five pages, each with different themes. The following page provides an overview of the findings and presents ten design principles that were produced as an output. Following this, there are three thematic inquiries that provide deeper dives into prominent themes that emerged from initial analysis.

As you review the findings, reflect on the following:

1. How do these findings relate to your own experiences of online professional learning?
2. How might a course designer utilise the design principles? Are there any you disagree with based on your experiences or perceptions of oTPD?
4. How can online TPD programmes do a better job of being evidence-based? What stands in the way? What are the current areas of strength that they can build on?
